[h1]SPACE RACER — 32-car anti-grav racing in CS2[/h1]

[b]Up to 64 players race at once in two arenas on eight original tracks.[/b] You get boost pads, banked bends, jump ramps, a spiral loop and spin attacks, and the podium collects real CS2 kills. Retro pixel art and chiptune music throughout. No server plugins: the whole game runs inside the map.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How a round works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pick your car[/b] in the lobby while the camera circles the next track. After that the picker stays out of the way: type [b]!car[/b] to bring it back.
[*][b]Line up on the grid.[/b] Everyone on T or CT races together. With 33–64 players there are two arenas on two different tracks, each with the same T/CT split. You can still swap cars during the 10-second countdown, up to 3 seconds before the lights.
[*][b]Race 2–3 laps.[/b] Three beeps, then GO. Hold W too early and you stall.
[*][b]The top 3 survive.[/b] Everyone else takes a CS2 death, and those kills go to the podium (50% / 30% / 20%). If you wreck someone with an attack, that kill is yours on the spot.
[*][b]Your team scores with every place.[/b] The CS2 round goes to the side with more cars in the top 8, counted over both arenas.
[*][b]Rounds are locked.[/b] Latecomers watch: they can switch cars and arenas, and they race next round.
[/list]

[h2]Four cars[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]HORNET (Drift Charge)[/b]: light and sharp. Drifting fills charge fast, and its spin attack is cheap.
[*][b]METEOR (Ram)[/b]: heavy and fast in a straight line. Bumps hurt the other car, not you.
[*][b]COMET (Afterburn)[/b]: the biggest charge tank and the strongest boost, and boost pads last longer.
[*][b]BASTION (Slipstream)[/b]: the toughest shield. Drafting fills its charge twice as fast and repairs it.
[/list]
Each car has its own ability on [b]E[/b]: the Hornet's [b]Phase Dash[/b] slips sideways through traffic, the Meteor's [b]Shockwave[/b] shoves everyone nearby across the road, the Comet's [b]Nova Burn[/b] dumps all its charge into an overdrive that leaves a trail of fire, and the Bastion's [b]Aegis[/b] throws up a shell nothing can hurt and tops up teammates' shields.
Each car shows its speed, acceleration, turning, shield and charge on the picker cards. Your choice is remembered between rounds. The light cars win a little more often; the heavy ones wreck more rivals.

[h2]Eight original tracks[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Solar Oval[/b]: a huge speedway at sunset, with an elevated back straight, a chicane and a tube.
[*][b]Hairpin Harbour[/b]: long straights with two big jumps and banked hairpins climbing terrace by terrace, then down through the harbour tube.
[*][b]Crosswire[/b]: a neon figure-8. One pass spirals a full loop round the crossing while the other threads through underneath it.
[*][b]Null Gap[/b]: narrow, rail-less roads over the void that roll like a roller coaster. Mind the gap.
[*][b]Switchback Spire[/b]: four stacked switchbacks to the summit, a long banked sweep, then a dive through a tube.
[*][b]Ringworld[/b]: a figure-8 among giant rings. One pass is a bridge high above, and the other runs under it through a tube.
[*][b]Skyway[/b]: a stadium high over a sea of clouds, with steep banking and a jump on each straight. Hit the jumps fast or drop in.
[*][b]Helix[/b]: a 1.5-turn spiral climbing into the sky, then a banked dive home through a tube.
[/list]
Each track has its own pixel-art sky, scenery, start gantry, sector gates and billboards. Lap records are kept while the map runs and shown in the lobby.

[h2]The racing[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shield is your health and charge is your boost.[/b] When your shield hits zero you are wrecked and out.
[*][b]Boost pads[/b] give free speed. [b]Charge platforms[/b], on the slower lines, refill charge and shield.
[*][b]Drafting[/b] behind another car trickles in charge (twice as fast behind a teammate, so convoys pay off), and [b]drifting[/b] (Space) fills it a little.
[*][b]Spin attack[/b] (Mouse 2) hits anyone alongside and shoves them across the road, often into the rail. A heavier car shoves harder. It costs charge.
[*][b]The Pulse[/b] is a wave that sweeps from the back of the field to the front once a lap and tops up everyone it passes.
[*][b]Banked bends[/b] grip harder. [b]Jump ramps[/b] throw you over open gaps. [b]Rail-less sections[/b] let you fall off: that costs shield and a reset.
[*]The race ends when everyone is home, 30 seconds after the winner, or at 5 minutes (ranked by distance).
[/list]

[h2]Made for CS2 servers[/h2]
[list]
[*]It runs entirely as a cs_script inside the map: no plugins and nothing else to install.
[*]Up to 64 players. CS2 bots race too, using the map's own driving AI ([i]bot_quota[/i] fills the grid).
[*]Real kills on the kill feed and scoreboard, and real T vs CT round wins.
[*]It has its own AFK check: idle drivers sit out until they press a key or type !back.
[*]CS2's chat and kill feed stay readable. The HUD keeps clear of them.
[/list]

[h2]Retro all the way[/h2]
Pixel-art tracks, cities, asteroids and skies in one 32-colour palette. The pixel-font HUD shows your position, lap, live team score, a minimap with every car, lap timer and best lap, standings, shield, charge and speed, plus a results board with times and gaps. CRT scanlines are optional. All sound effects are chiptune, and there are four original music pieces.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]W / S[/b]: accelerate / brake and reverse
[*][b]A / D[/b]: steer (and choose a car while the car picker is up)
[*][b]E[/b]: your car's ability (and confirm your car on the picker)
[*][b]R[/b]: bring the car picker back
[*][b]Mouse 1[/b]: boost (uses charge)
[*][b]Mouse 2[/b]: spin attack (uses charge)
[*][b]Space[/b]: drift (fills charge)
[*]While watching: [b]Mouse 1 / Mouse 2[/b] next / previous car, [b]E[/b] next arena
[/list]
[b]Chat (! or /):[/b] !car (the car picker), !car 1-4 or a car's name (pick straight away), !cam (high / far / near camera), !tilt (camera lean on/off), !crt (scanlines on/off), !music, !spec, !afk, !back, !help (controls on your screen)

[h2]Server notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The map sets the cvars it needs: respawn on, teammates are enemies (no teamkill penalties), buying off, radar off.
[*]On servers with plugins, turn these off for this map: AFK kickers, no-respawn and spawn-protection plugins, teamkill punishment, plugins that control rounds or round cvars, loadout or knife plugins, and noblock / anti-stuck / third-person plugins. Also check that no other plugin uses the chat commands above.
[/list]
